South Coast Rx Physiotherapy Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
South Coast Rx Physiotherapy (SCRx) is a privately operated physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation practice founded by Cameron Brew and Daniel Rebolledo. With clinics in Portsmouth and Fareham, the business provides physiotherapy, musculoskeletal treatment, sports injury rehabilitation, post-surgical recovery and performance-focused services.

The redesigned homepage positions SCRx as an evidence-led physiotherapy and performance healthcare provider. The design focuses on communicating its active rehabilitation approach, improving service discovery and making clinic locations and appointment options easier to access.
2. Main Improvements
1. Stronger Hero Section and Conversion Direction
- Introduced a clearer headline communicating SCRx's physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation expertise.
- Added benefit-focused supporting messaging explaining who the clinic helps and what it offers.
- Improved the visibility of booking calls to action and relevant trust signals.
Problem addressed: The previous hero section did not communicate the clinic's value proposition strongly enough or provide a clear direction for visitors to book an appointment.
2. Improved Service Discovery and Information Hierarchy
- Organised physiotherapy, sports rehabilitation and performance services into clearer categories.
- Improved the presentation of individual treatments through structured service sections and cards.
- Made it easier for visitors to identify relevant treatments based on their rehabilitation or performance needs.
Problem addressed: The wide range of services lacked a sufficiently clear information hierarchy, making treatment discovery more difficult.
3. Clearer Clinic Locations and Local Conversion
- Gave greater prominence to the Portsmouth and Fareham clinic locations.
- Improved the presentation of clinic addresses and available parking information.
- Made contact and appointment booking options easier to identify.
- Highlighted location-specific information to help visitors choose the appropriate clinic.
Problem addressed: Clinic information was not prominent enough, making it harder for visitors to identify their nearest location and access appointment options.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: Established a professional, energetic and sport-focused appearance reflecting SCRx's active rehabilitation and performance positioning.
- Homepage Layout: Improved section hierarchy, service organisation and the visibility of important conversion elements.
- User Experience: Simplified treatment discovery and made booking and clinic information easier to access.
- Content Consistency: Presented physiotherapy, rehabilitation and performance services within a more cohesive structure.
- Local Accessibility: Strengthened the presentation of both clinic locations, addresses and parking information.

Project Focus: Stronger service positioning, easier treatment discovery and improved local appointment conversion.
