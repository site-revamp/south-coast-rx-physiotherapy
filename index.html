Get to know South Coast Rx

Your recovery should move you forward.

At South Coast Rx Physiotherapy, we believe physiotherapy should help you do more than simply manage discomfort. It should help you regain confidence, improve your movement and return to the activities that matter to you.

Our approach combines specialist physiotherapy with strength and conditioning. We focus on understanding your needs, identifying what is limiting your movement and developing a rehabilitation pathway around your individual goals.

From everyday movement to sporting performance, our aim is to help you build the strength and confidence to keep progressing.

Performance & prevention

Go beyond recovery. Work towards your potential.

Whether you are an athlete, an active individual or someone looking to improve physical performance, South Coast Rx offers services that support movement, preparation and progression.

Performance-focused services combine assessment, movement analysis and strength and conditioning to help identify opportunities to improve how you move and perform.

  • Performance movement screening
  • Gait analysis & running MOT
  • Strength and conditioning
  • Pitchside physiotherapy
  • Sports event physiotherapy
  • First aid support
